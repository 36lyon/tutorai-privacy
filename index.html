TutorAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 | App operator: RAHFAS

This Privacy Policy explains how RAHFAS ("we", "us" or "our"), the operator of TutorAI, collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the TutorAI mobile application and related services.

Important: When you use TutorAI's AI features, the questions, prompts, conversations and study text you submit may be sent to our backend and the AI provider selected by the backend. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information that is not needed for your learning task.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: Information associated with your TutorAI account, including your email address, authentication identifiers and session information managed through Supabase.
  • Learning content: Questions, prompts, chat messages, answers, study notes, topics, subjects, examination preferences and other material you submit to TutorAI learning features.
  • Learning activity and progress: Practice and examination activity, scores, correct and total answer counts, subject or topic information and completion dates. Some progress information is stored locally on your device.
  • Files and scanned questions: When you select a PDF or text file, TutorAI extracts readable text on your device. When you scan a question, Google ML Kit text recognition is used on the device to extract text. The extracted text may be sent to an AI service when you request analysis or an explanation. The inspected scanning workflow does not show the original scanned image being uploaded to TutorAI's backend.
  • Voice input: If you use the microphone feature, your speech is processed by the speech-recognition service available on your device to produce a text transcript. The transcript may be submitted to TutorAI. Audio processing depends on your device and its configured recognition service.
  • Payment and subscription information: If you purchase TutorAI Premium, information processed includes your email address, selected plan, transaction reference, amount, currency, payment status and payment date. Paystack processes the payment. The inspected payment flow does not show TutorAI collecting or storing your full payment-card number or card PIN.
  • Technical and support information: Information you provide when contacting support and limited technical information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Provide AI tutoring, explanations and practice questions.
  • Generate summaries, quizzes and study materials.
  • Read selected study files and recognise scanned questions.
  • Record and display learning activity and progress.
  • Retrieve examination questions and academic content.
  • Process and verify Premium subscriptions.
  • Respond to support requests and maintain security.
  • Meet applicable legal and accounting obligations.

3. AI Features and Third-Party AI Providers

TutorAI's backend is configured to use Google Gemini as its usual AI provider. Depending on backend configuration, request type and service availability, requests may also be processed by DeepSeek or Groq as fallback providers. Ollama is supported when the backend is explicitly configured to use it.

When you use an AI feature, the information needed to answer your request, such as your question, prompt, conversation context or extracted study-note text, may be transmitted to the configured AI provider.

These providers process information under their own terms, privacy policies and applicable service arrangements. We do not control their independent privacy practices.

4. Other Service Providers and Sharing

Depending on the feature and configuration, TutorAI uses:

  • Supabase: Account authentication and database services.
  • Google Gemini: AI processing.
  • DeepSeek: Optional AI reasoning and fallback processing.
  • Groq: Optional AI fallback processing.
  • Ollama: Optional AI provider when specifically configured.
  • ALOC: Examination-question and academic-content retrieval. Search information sent includes examination type, subject, year, country and requested question limit.
  • Paystack: Payment initiation and transaction verification for Premium subscriptions.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device text recognition for scanned questions.
  • Device speech-recognition service: Speech-to-text functionality. Processing depends on your device and configured service.

We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the Service, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Storage, Security and International Processing

Some information is stored locally on your device, including certain learning-progress and preference data. Account and payment-related records are handled through Supabase and the relevant backend services.

Information sent to external providers may be processed in countries other than your country of residence.

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated backend requests and server-side handling of sensitive service credentials. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain accounts, support learning features, administer subscriptions, resolve disputes, protect the Service and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Retention periods may differ by information type and service provider. Third-party providers may retain information under their own policies and contractual arrangements.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can sign out through the app. Signing out does not delete your account or associated information.

TutorAI currently provides account sign-out, but the app code reviewed for this Policy did not show an in-app account-deletion function.

To request deletion of your TutorAI account and associated personal information, email:

rahfas003@gmail.com

Deleting the app or signing out does not itself submit an account-deletion request. Local information may also need to be cleared from your device separately.

8. Children's Privacy

TutorAI is an educational application and may be used by students. Parents and legal guardians should guide younger users about the information they submit to AI features.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is not appropriate or authorised, contact us at rahfas003@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

You may exercise applicable rights by contacting us. We may ask for information needed to verify your request and may be unable to fulfil requests where a legal exception applies.
